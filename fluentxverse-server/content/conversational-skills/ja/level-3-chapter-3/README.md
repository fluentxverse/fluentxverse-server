## Content Standard

English instruction with Japanese support, six vocabulary items, Part II
discussion, Part III pronunciation, two language tips, six Step A items and
four Step B questions with answer keys. Speaking Apply activities have ten
alternating lines. Listening passages stay short and tutor-only. Reading
missions use fresh written evidence kept visible throughout the roleplay.
All guides use the existing numbered structure, with tutor-first roleplays,
useful language, and the fixed range/accuracy/fluency feedback guide.

Role assignments are direct: "You are Emma. Your tutor is Leo." Do not append
alternate-character options or personal-information disclaimers. The same
editorial correction is applied to the sixteen authored Chapter 1/2 lessons
through `scripts/correct-direct-roleplay-copy.ts`, without rebuilding their
content, replacing their images, or changing their publication states.

## Story Continuity

The coffee follows Chapter 2's invitation. Work deadlines explain the lack of
time to talk. Claire gets concrete help during a cafe rush and hosts dinner.
Emma's assumption about a work lunch creates the interpersonal conflict;
Aya is Leo's bakery coworker. Emma apologizes for guessing and going quiet,
while Leo clarifies the facts. Both agree to ask instead of assume.

Nao gets practical support with six labels and visitor questions. Rehearsal
is Thursday after 6 p.m.; Claire checks labels Friday; the exhibition ends
Saturday at 5 p.m., packing runs until 6, and the celebration is at 6:30.
The final invitation is for the following Sunday after 5 p.m.
