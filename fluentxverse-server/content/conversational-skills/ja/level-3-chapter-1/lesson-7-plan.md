# Lesson 7 Proposal: The Poster Went Out

Planning only. The live Lesson 7 has not been revised to this proposal.

## Continuity From Lesson 6

- Saturday morning: a burst pipe closes Kamo House cafe.
- Nao initially fears cancellation. Claire confirms the Gojo Art Room for Sunday, 3-5 p.m., replacing the cafe and the 2 p.m. start.
- Nao asks Emma to tell Leo to call her former pottery teacher that morning. Claire has informed the other guests.
- The old poster appears online. Nao asks Emma whether she listened. Lesson 6 does not establish why the post appeared.

## Reading Objective

Read a short message thread and a corrected notice to identify the order of events and give the latest confirmed information.

## Proposed Reveal

Emma scheduled the original poster before the venue changed. It published automatically while she was passing the update to Leo. The record does not support Nao's assumption that Emma deliberately ignored her. Emma still accepts responsibility for not stopping the scheduled post or checking it in time.

Do not reuse the older draft's claim that Emma had not seen the update on a train: Lesson 6 now has her listening and relaying it before the cliffhanger.

## Lesson Parts

- Learn: latest update, scheduled post, old address, correct a mistake, before, after. Include Part II discussion and a short basic pronunciation contrast.
- Reading Tip: use timestamps and before/after; distinguish what a message proves from what someone assumes. Do not make past perfect the main beginner-level exercise target.
- Apply: read the short timestamped thread, answer evidence-based questions, and explain why the post appeared.
- Step A: put events in order or choose before/after.
- Step B: compare the original and corrected notices; select details supported by the latest version.
- Challenge 1: read a fresh corrected notice, then help a visitor holding the old poster find the right venue and time. Tutor speaks first; student refers to the notice rather than memorizing Lesson 6.
- Useful language candidates: According to the message, ... / This happened before ... / The old poster says ..., but the latest notice says ... / The correct details are ...
- Challenge 2: optional general discussion about online invitations and misunderstandings.
- Feedback: existing fixed rubric and six-step guide. Base objective achievement mainly on reading evidence in Challenge 1.

Use the same tutor-guide structure as Lessons 1-4 and 6. Keep answer keys in tutor guidance. Record actual mistakes in the existing assessment controls and notes. No new images until requested.

## Ending Toward Lesson 8

The correct notice fixes the public information, but Nao remains hurt. Emma needs to speak with her directly, apologize, and agree on practical next steps. Lesson 8 should use a dialogue, not the older draft's compulsory monologue.
